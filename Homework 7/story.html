My Story

About Me

My name is Addie and I was born and raised here in Missoula. On father's day in 2018, me, my sister, and my dad all took his car and went out to hike Morell Falls near Seeley. We got lost twice, all while the Beastie Boys were playing on the radio. We then gave up because it had been hours, so we decided to drive home. On the way home, we got not one, but TWO flat tires. My uncle ended up picking us up and we all got pizza after.
